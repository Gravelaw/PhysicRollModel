Why the band appears within seconds. Radius growth follows the radial heat integral, so right after a spray change the stripe grows at 2*alpha*factor*dq/(rho*c) whatever the radial distribution; short-lived bands arise because the stripe crosses the manifest threshold within seconds to tens of seconds and fades over about a minute after the zone returns, not because the roll skin responds fast.

Open discrepancy. The 20 s first-order flatness response identified on a plant (Dohmae & Okamura 2012) is faster than this model's decay; possible causes (none modelled): bite heat depending on the roll surface temperature, emulsion-level heat transfer, per-revolution effects.

Limitations and sources

  • Screening result on unvalidated, uncalibrated models; no plant validation is claimed.
  • The score is an ordinal evidence tally (supports 3/2/1 minus contradictions 6/2/1 for strong/moderate/weak), not a probability and not a certified cause.
  • The chatter model is linear: it cannot predict limit-cycle amplitude, mark severity or strip break, and a forced response at an unstable operating point is formal only.
  • Strip spans are lumped springs (inaccurate near the span wave resonance c/(2L)).
  • Circular (Hitchcock) roll flattening is not valid toward foil gauges; thinner passes need supplied bite sensitivities (model_options.bite_response_override).
  • The bite-damping closure (fraction beta of transit time) is a declared heuristic; results without it (beta = 0) are reported alongside.
  • Friction is an input unless tribology.use_computed_friction is set; the friction-speed closure of bite_tribology is an unfitted placeholder whose slope, not level, is used.
  • Fifth-octave regeneration (backup-roll marking) is screened kinematically, not simulated in time; the antisymmetric mode frequency is an undamped lumped estimate.
  • Spray coverage is a geometric footprint count; the phase-1 thermal growth is a free-expansion upper bound with no axial conduction (the v2 width_model solves the roll temperature field instead).
  • The lengthwise physics (roll thermal stripes, shape chain, coil ridges, troughs) rests on planner-derived and memory formulas flagged in each result; no plant validation.

Known disagreements in the literature

  • Effect of friction on chatter is disputed: Yarita (high friction worse), Chefneux (low friction worse), Kimura et al. 2003 (an optimum band). This model gives whatever its slab-derived sensitivities give; see the lever table.
  • Effect of tension is disputed: Heidari (little effect) vs Zeng, Zang & Gao 2016 (higher tension lowers the critical speed).
  • The lumped-spring strip span is inaccurate near the span axial wave resonance c/(2L) (about 640 Hz for a 4 m aluminium span); Niroomand et al. 2015 compare lumped and wave span models.
