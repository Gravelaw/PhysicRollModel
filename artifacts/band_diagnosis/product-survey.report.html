Limitations and sources

  • Synthetic: one stated mill per gauge class (the demo mills), not any plant; the products differ in gauge, flow stress, tension and speed only.
  • Inputs are literature values from the product catalogue (a survey of excerpts, patents, standards previews and vendor or trade pages); each row carries the read status.
  • Assumed values are labelled per row (flow stress without a published as-rolled strength, reduction without a published span, the tension fractions, friction 0.03, the mill).
  • Doubled (pack-rolled) final passes are not modelled: no bite, only per-sheet bite-free thresholds.
  • Roll bites below about 60 um come from the experimental mattress model (order of magnitude); below 20 um exit gauge or above 50 % reduction from its unvalidated extended domain. The force range used for the credibility mark is reported for battery-foil passes only.
  • Chatter numbers are open-gap linear screening values; above the span-wave limit they are flagged not reliable.
  • Nothing here is validated against plant data; rankings compare model sensitivities on a synthetic mill, not plant defect rates.
  • Literature survey only: every value is quoted from three research reports, not measured.
  • No plant validation: a value inside a published span is not evidence that a pass is good.
  • Vendor and trade pages are low-confidence; several standards were read from previews only.
  • Finished-product tensile strength is not the rolling flow stress; that comparison is an indicator only.
  • Spans mix minima, typical values and ranges from different sources; read the basis.
