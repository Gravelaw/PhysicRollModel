They do not choose an aluminium constitutive card or erase current domain restrictions. The [thermal handoff](thermal-numerical-handoff.md) separates conservative rejection of the current closure from revised numerical/physical formulations.

Public material-testing fundamentals improve the data request; they cannot reconstruct absent measurements. [Targeted P1/P2 access priorities](p1-p2-access-priorities.md) distinguish blockers from useful but deferrable reading.

The new [operational-phase requirements](operational-phase-review.md) make recipe-versus-actual trajectories, material tracking and per-model eligibility part of the core architecture. They use primary papers and OEM technical sources as evidence, not a universal invented startup sequence. A steady model sampled along a ramp is not automatically a dynamic simulator. This requirement enters P1/P3/P4 and constrains P5 objectives before appearing in the UI.
