## Corrections and clarifications sent to the author

1. **Correct R014's locator.** The linked 2024 arXiv v1 discusses output-dependent steady state in **§4.4, Figure 6**, not §3.7. The claim itself is supported: near-constant total force/torque precede stabilization of internal stress/strain fields in that FE example. No plant dwell time follows from this example. [Primary preprint v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.03242v1).
2. **State the no-slip winding kinematics.** Write `omega = v/R` only for compatible signed conventions, no slip at the winding/unwinding point, local strip speed `v`, and instantaneous coil contact radius `R`. Its derivative in the proposal is correct under those assumptions. A work-roll speed or a strip speed on the opposite side of a reducing bite cannot substitute for that local speed. Gear-ratio transformations remain separate. This is a mathematical qualification of the proposed contract, not an OEM recipe.
3. **Credit and extend existing representation.** The baseline already includes phase-tagged observations/ranges, static reel bookkeeping and unavailable material-coordinate fields. O0 should extend these contracts rather than introduce a competing representation. The code evidence below establishes precisely what exists and what remains missing.

## Scientific acceptance of the proposed contracts

The proposed phase, equipment, material, control-mode, recipe and validity dimensions are appropriately separate. Actual process evidence determines a phase; commands alone cannot establish it. Output-specific eligibility and adaptation gates remain independent. A force prediction may be eligible while a residual-stress prediction or parameter update remains unsupported. A dynamic simulator also need not reproduce the plant controller merely to replay measured boundaries.

The changing-coil warning is correct. For an open control volume about a fixed reel axis, external torque balances angular-momentum storage plus net outward transport. `d(J*omega)/dt` alone is therefore insufficient when material enters or leaves. Any cancellation between the changing-inertia term and transported angular momentum depends on the stated winding geometry and incoming velocity. Do not add a `Jdot*omega` term to the existing arithmetic helper, or delete one from a new derivation, without establishing that balance. The proposal properly leaves the derivation and measured losses/inertias as O2 prerequisites.

The material-coordinate requirement is also sound: local speed integration is meaningful only with sensor location, signed direction, timebase and slip uncertainty. Dynamic span storage/elastic stretch must be included when relevant. A reference mass coordinate or explicitly transformed reference length prevents entry/exit reduction, crops and reversal from corrupting parcel identity. Time samples through a bite do not represent repeated rolling of the same parcel. The ideal coil-length formula is dimensionally correct and its winding assumptions are stated.

The acceptance matrix covers the essential falsifiers: no plateau, interrupted tracking, direction changes, loss of tension boundary conditions, estimator error, steady/dynamic limits, signed energy and grouped independent validation. Recommend retaining two explicit schema fixtures when implementation begins: (a) one material parcel passing different sensors during an acceleration, with changing delay, and (b) the same material batch undergoing two real passes versus two samples from one pass. These are proposed verification cases, not evidence of current capability.

No numerical plant thresholds, controller gains or accuracy tolerances can be inferred from the reviewed vendor examples. The plan correctly allows O0 architecture and synthetic verification now, while making plant-domain qualification depend on measured synchronized time series and declared uncertainty. No further scientific blocker was identified within this review scope.
