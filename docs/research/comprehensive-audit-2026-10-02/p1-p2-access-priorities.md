2 October 2026. Planning baseline `caec37ef86a8b78e1eb88292ea6368bfb64bcbdc`. Read with [roadmap](roadmap.md), [implementation traceability](implementation-traceability.md), [benchmark review](benchmark-independent-review.md) and the [machine-readable access supplement](p1-p2-access-source-reviews.json).

## Decision

P1 can advance through explicit quantity contracts, analytic limits, conservation and refinement without obtaining every historical paper first. P2 can support a deliberately conditional AA5052 replay, subject to those contracts. Neither becomes physical validation by replacing a paywalled source with a readable paper. The remaining critical P2 gap is source-specific information about R062, including initial material history, strain/stress conventions, radius wording, speed reference and friction provenance. That paper is already accessible; downloading more generic literature cannot establish those missing facts.

This follow-up targeted nine existing identities. It upgrades three from metadata to relevant-body review (R007, R013, R050), one from metadata to official-body review (R121), and one from unavailable to official-body review (R051). R001/R002/R078 remain primary-abstract only; R008 remains metadata only. These are proposed review-level access updates for integration, not a new global corpus count. Full-text availability does not mean exhaustive reading. Figures and rendered mathematical notation were not freshly visually checked.

## Evidence gained and its practical limits

**R121, units.** The official English document is the ninth edition, PDF title version 4.01; a `version=7.0` download parameter is not a seventh edition. Relevant printed pages 129,133–136,144–146 were read. Table 4 distinguishes N, Pa, J and W; Table 5 and adjacent text distinguish torque from energy despite shared dimensions. The temperature relation uses a 273.15 offset, while intervals have equal K/°C magnitudes. This supports explicit quantity names alongside units; it does not settle radius/diameter or per-roll/whole-mill scope. [Official BIPM brochure](https://www.bipm.org/en/publications/si-brochure).

**R051, contact heat.** The exact 2024 theory page distinguishes the frictional-work conversion fraction from the fractions entering the two bodies. Its heat fluxes use outward surface signs, and the interface has no heat capacity. The contact coefficient multiplies temperature difference before surface integration. For this project's audit, a per-area coefficient in W/(m² K), lumped conductance in W/K and heat power in W need separate fields; equivalent numerical values are not interchangeable. This is model documentation, not aluminium experimental evidence. [Official 2024 theory](https://docs.software.vt.edu/abaqusv2024/English/SIMACAETHERefMap/simathe-c-fricheatgen.htm).

**R007, a reserve experiment.** The paper's AW2024/5083 study uses 125 mm diameter rolls,173.4 mm/s peripheral speed and no applied strip tension. Sections 2.2.3 and 3.2 explain the adopted friction and material testing. In particular, 0.1 is a chosen friction value after a 0.06–0.2 sensitivity study, not independent tribometry. The extracted Eq. 1 signs require rendered verification. This source can improve a future strain-field case, but cannot supply AA5052 history or remove R062 friction leakage. [Identified coauthor-upload text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/291527264_Deformations_and_velocities_during_the_cold_rolling_of_aluminium_alloys); the [official PDF](https://mit.imt.si/izvodi/mit161/misovic.pdf) timed out in this attempt.

**R050, plastic heating.** The recovered HAL file is a 2020 journal pre-proof deposited as v1 in 2024, not a verified copy of the final journal layout. Section 4.2 distinguishes plastic work-rate conversion from boundary heat loss; Appendix A compares thermocouple wire sizes. Tables 2–4 identify aging-dependent AA6016-T4 properties. This makes it useful for designing a synchronized mechanical/thermal material experiment. The manuscript does not provide AA5052 rolling calibration, roll-strip thermal conductance, or a universal conversion fraction. Its fit comparisons must not be relabelled independent holdouts. [Official HAL manuscript](https://hal.science/hal-04699292v1/file/R2.20.pdf).

**R013, a promising alternative with its own ambiguities.** The current publisher HTML describes AA1050 rolling, a semi-empirical flowline model, FE comparisons and observed distortion patterns. Data are available only on request. The material constants are sourced from another paper. In §5, the printed input gauges and reductions disagree:

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| B |0.5625→0.4615|17.9556%|17.24%|
| D |2.53→1.81|28.4585%|28.09%|

These arithmetic checks are an independent reading of the published text, not simulations. Cases A/B are numerical comparisons; C/D/E are experimental distortion cases with the same incoming gauge, not a multipass route. The page states a minor typographical republication but gives no correction date in the accessed statement. Before extraction, pin that version, resolve the values, visually inspect equations and obtain distortion data. [Official corrected article](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4701/15/1/11).

## What replacements can close

An accessible primary formulation such as the already reviewed R012 preprint can support a reproducible slab/field derivation in a clearly declared approximation. R005/R006 can support contact/flattening comparisons within their reviewed domains. The forthcoming fundamentals review can supply independently checked continuum, plasticity, thermal and numerical definitions. These alternatives can close a **generic method explanation** gap when the chosen implementation is mapped explicitly to them. They cannot establish that a historical method was reproduced exactly, or make steel/idealized examples into aluminium plant calibration.

Similarly, R007/R013/R118 provide distinct strain/kinematics experiments; R134 provides a separate force/torque table. They are useful reserve studies with their own unresolved material, friction or measurement conventions. They cannot repair R062's missing data by analogy. Requesting a lawful author copy or raw data is the appropriate next access action where exact reproduction matters. No author was contacted by this task.

## Work that remains possible now

1. **Freeze the P1 semantic contract.** Separate force from stress, roll radius from diameter, signed tension power from rolling torque, per-roll from combined torque, strain coordinates from effective hardening state, and flux from integrated power. Independently unit-check each adapter. A unit conversion is permitted only after the quantity is identified.
2. **Implement the numerical handoff later in Codex cloud.** Preserve the zero-source I01 counterexample and independently derive the intended stream/storage thermal problem before choosing a remedy. Require finite-step bounds, entropy accounting consistent with that problem, refinement, and energy closure. No plastic-heating paper can repair a zero-source discretization error by changing a coefficient.
3. **Freeze P2 data lineage before a new fit.** R062 observations, source predictions, derivations and assumptions must have different fields. Run fixed-assumption comparisons first. If justified, fit one global effective initial-hardening parameter to pass 1 force, leave pass 1 slip diagnostic and use passes 2/3 only as previously seen within-chain conditional checks. A bounded fit does not measure prestrain. Published friction may already encode the targets.
4. **Choose the next independent data request by failure mode.** If force and slip cannot be reconciled within declared assumptions, prioritize independent initial flow curves and friction/speed/tension provenance over adding more fit parameters. If thermal behavior is the question, obtain synchronized temperatures and boundary conditions rather than infer them from force agreement. If the scalar model is adequate but field outputs matter, extract a separate R007/R013/R118 kinematics study.

Do not delay P1 verification for R003 hot-rolling approximations, R010/R056/R057 foil/mixed-lubrication refinements, R065–R074 microstructure/damage models, or R141/R142 thermal/FE archives unless the selected output and method actually depend on them. Those are later science/access workstreams, not justification to widen the immediate conditional model. This prioritization leaves their corpus gaps visible; it does not declare their contents reviewed.

## Stop conditions and closure

Stop any exact-paper reproduction claim at a missing body or unresolved transcription. Stop benchmark promotion at ambiguous quantities, mismatched executable adapters, fitted parameters masking unit choices, undocumented resets, or target reuse presented as independence. Stop numerical promotion at I01-style finite-step violations even when the first-law residual is tiny. Source availability, test counts and attractive fitted residuals do not override these gates.

This access supplement is ready for ledger integration. It closes five access-depth gaps at the stated limited reading depth. It leaves source-specific AA5052 identification, licensed primary retrieval, raw experimental data, figure/equation visual checks and U001 explicitly unresolved. Deployment options remain open; this document authorizes neither implementation nor plant operation.
