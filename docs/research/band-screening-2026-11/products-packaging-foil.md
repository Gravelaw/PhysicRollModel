**Why each alloy** (largely vendor statements, consistent with the EN 546-2 strength ranking):
- **1235 / 1145 / 1200:** softest, best rollability to 6 µm.
- **8079 (Fe 0.7–1.3, Si 0.05–0.3):** more strength and elongation at thin gauge than 1xxx.
- **8011 / 8011A (Al-Fe-Si):** general-purpose; the hard-temper lidding alloy.
- **8021 / 8021B (Fe 1.2–1.7):** highest O-temper elongation (≥12 % at 45 µm), hence cold forming.
- **8006 (Al-Fe-Mn):** strongest (O-temper Rm 80–140 MPa), for containers and down-gauged household foil.
- **3xxx:** rigidity in containers.
- **5052:** appears only for single-rolled technical foil [S18 V].

## 2. Rolling route notes

**Foil stock.**
- Kerth: 0.5–0.7 mm, annealed dead-soft before foil rolling, from 5–8 mm hot band or cast strip. Stock tolerance ±5 % along length and ±1 % across width, to avoid edge corrugation [S1 F].
- Hindalco: 0.2–0.6 mm ±5 %, tempers "M" (as-rolled, Rm 125–180 MPa), H14 or H16. Flatness <20 I-units, wedge ≤0.7 %; made from DC ingot and concast [S23 V].
- Twin-roll cast is described as limited to pure or low-alloy grades [S5 F]. It is used industrially for 1235, 8011, 8079, 8006 and 8021 with a homogenisation step [S3 F, S31 S].

**Household foil, 450 µm to 10.5 µm** [S2 F].

| Pass | Gauge µm | Reduction | Speed m/min |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 450 → 215 | 52 % | 600 |
| 2 | 215 → 100 | 53 % | 900 |
| 3 | 100 → 47 | 53 % | 900 |
| 4 | 47 → 23 | 51 % | 900 |
| 5 (doubled) | 2×23 → 2×10.5 | 54 % | 600 |

**Converter foil, 450 µm to 6.5 µm** [S2 F].

| Pass | Gauge µm | Reduction | Speed m/min |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 450 → 220 | 51 % | 700 |
| 2 | 220 → 110 | 50 % | 1000 |
| 3 | 110 → 55 | 50 % | 1000 |
| 4 | 55 → 26 | 53 % | 1000 |
| 5 | 26 → 14 | 46 % | 1000 |
| 6 (doubled) | 2×14 → 2×6.5 | 54 % | 700 |

The patent's text says "400 µm" and omits 110; its table is as above.

**Industrial twin-roll-cast 8079 to 6.35 µm** [S3 F].
- 8 mm cast → 4 mm (50 %) → homogenise 580 °C / 8 h → 0.53 mm in four passes (about 40 % each) → anneal 450 °C / 4 h.
- 0.25 mm (53 %) → 100 µm (60 %) → 56 µm (44 %) → 14 µm in two passes (about 50 % each) → 2×14 → 2×6.35 (55 %).
- Speeds 175 / 325 / 560 m/min on the three foil mills; work rolls 240 mm.
- Final anneal 270 °C / 11 h. About 10 pinholes/dm² were reported.

**Alcoa Yennora, 1145** [S16 S].
- Exit gauges 29 → 14 → "13D" (2×6.5): 52 % and 54 %.
- Tension-speed AGC; loads 360–460 t; exit stress 4.6–5.0 kg/mm².
- Target 700 m/min, actual about 490–600.
- Coolant viscosity 1.9 (unit not shown), 38–55 °C.
- Chromed work rolls "5–7 Ra" and "14–16 Ra" (unit not shown, probably µin).
- Final anneal between 235 °C / 90 h and 285 °C / 2 h.

**Doubling and surfaces.** Roll-side faces are bright and the foil-to-foil faces matt; single-rolled foil is bright on both sides [S6 F, S26 F]. The matt face is made of slip steps across the rolling direction, and pinholes are local shear-through from deep matt-side valleys [S1 F].

**Separating.** Separation, trim and slitting happen in one operation at 300–600 m/min, with light-box inspection for porosity and roll holes [S1 F].

**Final anneal (O temper).**
- Slow heating, metal at 240–280 °C, up to 70 h depending on coil width and diameter, good ventilation, controlled cooling.
- Aims: dry, flat (no "heat pockets"), non-sticky foil.
- Dryness trades off against stickiness; double-bright foil is the most sticky-prone, and >99.5 % Al risks coarse grain [S1 F].
- H18 lidding foil is used as-rolled, without anneal [S1 F].

**Oil and rolls.**
- Kerosene base with fatty acid, alcohol or ester additives [S1 F]; alcohol 2–5 % and ester 1–3 % [S29 V].
- Oil 20–40 °C in roughing, 40–60 °C in finishing; rougher rolls for roughing, finer for finishing [S29 V].
- Roll roughness about equals foil roughness after one pass [S3 F, S27 S].

## 3. General foil-rolling reference

- **Per-pass reduction:** "about 50 %", with five passes to 18–20 µm [S1 F]; ≈50 % in an industrial 1200 trial from 0.4 mm [S27 S]; 46–54 % computed from [S2]; 44–60 % from [S3].
- **Closed gap:** roll contact begins at 0.1–0.2 mm [S1 F]; "<100 µm" [S2 F]; "~0.080 mm" [S4 F]. Roll force is ineffective for gauge below about 50 µm. Tension is the fine control and speed the coarse control (the "speed effect", through oil-film hydrodynamics) [S1 F, S4 F]. An Achenbach mill uses position control at 300 µm and constant force on the doubled final pass [S28 V].
- **Doubling threshold:** see disagreements.
- **Speeds:** last pass limited to about 600 m/min for lustre [S1 F]; 600–1000 m/min schedule values [S2 F]; mill design maxima 2000–2250 m/min [S28 V].
- **Mill types:** roughing 0.7 → 0.012 mm, intermediate 0.12 → 0.006 mm, finishing 0.05 → <0.006 mm [S28 V].

## 4. Quality items relevant to bands and streaks

From the EAFA surface catalogue [S7 F]:
- **Rolling stripes:** gloss side only, constant along the rolling direction. Cause: local work-roll wear, or rolls damaged by foreign material from casting or foil stock.
- **Heat stripes:** grey, straight to snaking. Cause: temperature or flatness differences across the width, or a locally overheated roll.
- **Gloss difference:** matt side only. Cause: unstable separating-oil application at doubling.
- **Broken matt:** bright spots on the matt side. Cause: too much deformation or too high speed in the doubled pass.
- **Chatter marks:** mill or grinder vibration.
- **Grooves:** unfavourable flatness profile.

Other requirements:
- **Flatness:** no numeric foil limit found. Kerth uses waviness W = 100·f/L % and notes stretching is practical only above about 100 µm [S1 F].
- **Wettability:** EN classes A (water), B (+10 % ethanol), C (+20 %), on to G; A–C acceptable [S11 F]. ASTM equivalent: dry-annealed A / B / C, or "slick" [S12 F].

## 5. Standards

- **EN 546-1:** covers 6–200 µm. Defines converter foil (6–200, single or double rolled), consumer foil (10–24) and container foil (single-rolled, 35–200) [S8 F].
- **EN 546-2:** 15 mm strip, 50 or 100 mm gauge length; Table 1 (O and H18 by gauge band), Table 2 (container tempers) [S9 F].
- **EN 546-3:** single spot ±6 % for single-rolled, ±8 % for double-rolled. Lot average ±6 / 5 / 4 % (single) and ±8 / 6 / 4 % (double) for ≤3 t, 3–10 t, >10 t. Width ±1 mm up to 1000 mm, ±2 mm above. Weighing is the referee method [S10 F].
- **EN 546-4:** pinholes are <0.2 mm in 6–20 µm foil; roll holes are >0.2 mm and periodic. Light box 1000–1500 lux, ignore <0.02 mm. Limits are by agreement. Burst test for consumer foil, Erichsen for container foil [S11 F].
- **ASTM B479-19:** annealed, ≥98 % Al, 6.4 µm to <150 µm; alloys 1100, 1145, 1235, 8011, 8079, 8111. Thickness ±10 % per roll. Tensile by agreement. Pinholes by B926, with no numeric limit (≥50 µm "virtually free") [S12 F].
- **ASTM B373:** capacitor foil, 4.3–12.7 µm, 1145 / 1235 [S13 S].
- **GB/T 3198-2020:** local thickness ±6 % (ordinary) or ±5 % (high precision) at ≤9 µm, and ±5 % or ±4 % above. Lot average ±5 / 4 / 3 % [S14 F]. Pinhole limits, ultra-high / high / ordinary grade per m²: 500 / 1000 / 1500 at 6.0 µm; 150 / 300 / 500 at 6.5–7 µm; 100 / 150 / 200 at 7–9 µm; 20 / 50 / 100 at 9–12 µm; 0 / 5 / 10 at 20–40 µm [S14 V, third-party transcription].
- **GB/T 22638:** .1 thickness, .2 pinholes, .3 stickiness, .4 wetting tension, .5 wettability, .6 DC resistance, .7 heat-seal strength [S14 F].
- **YBB00152002-2015 (China, pharma lidding):** no pinholes >0.3 mm; ≤1/m² at 0.1–0.3 mm; burst ≥98 kPa; heat seal ≥7.0 N/15 mm to PVC [S15 S].
- **EN 573-3:** chemistry only; not read directly.

## 6. Disagreements between sources

- **Gauge at which doubling starts:** <20 µm, and necessary below 10–15 µm [S1]; <25 µm [S26]; <30 µm [S29]; ≤50 µm (Impol practice) [S18]. EN 546-4 lets double-rolled product run to 70 µm and single-rolled start at 35 µm.
- **Closed-gap onset:** 100–200 µm [S1], <100 µm [S2], about 80 µm [S4].
- **Blister lidding alloy:** 8011-H18 (Asian vendors) versus 1200 / 8079-H18 (Impol).
- **Thickness tolerance:** EN ±8 % and ASTM ±10 % versus vendor claims of ±3–5 %.
- **Pinholes at 6 µm:** <500–600/m² in vendor specs versus up to 1500/m² in GB ordinary grade.
- **Twin-roll cast suitability for 6–7 µm:** "restricted" [S5] versus routine but pinhole-prone [S3, S31].
- **Strip-foil burst strength:** two converters quote different minimums for the same gauge.
- **Wine-capsule foil gauge:** 6–10 µm versus 16–50 µm.
