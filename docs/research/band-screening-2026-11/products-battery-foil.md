# Battery and electronics aluminium foil: gauges, alloys, tempers, rolling routes

**Read tags:** [FT] page fetched and read (some truncated); [EX] long excerpts via search tool, not the whole document; [VP] vendor, producer or trade page; [MEM] memory, unverified. "Computed" means I calculated the reduction from the gauges. S-numbers refer to the source list.

**Most important flag:** the two schedules you hold (260→135→65→30 and 280→120→55→24 µm) come from the trade page S17. There the next pass is listed as 24×2→12×2 and 28×2→15×2, i.e. doubled. So 30 and 24 µm are doubling-entry gauges for one-side-bright foil, not single-sheet final gauges. The first-plant row prints "302→152", which I read as 30×2→15×2.

## Disagreements between sources

1. **1xxx strength.** EN 546-2 H18 minima are 135–140 MPa, Speira standard ≥165, GB/T 175–190, UACJ typical 180–185. One vendor page claims 1060 at 230–250 MPa and 1100 above 270 [VP S48], which fits only the high-strength variants.
2. **Passes and reductions.** Three to six foil passes; 29–64 % per pass. S12 says the industry norm is 5–6 passes from ≤0.24 mm stock.
3. **Final single-sheet speed.** 450 m/min (thermal softening argument) versus 1000–1200 m/min claimed.
4. **Minimum single-rolled gauge.** About 9 µm [EX S18], 12 µm on the Mino mill, UACJ sells 10 µm as bright/matte, yet 8 µm double-bright is reported in production [EX S21].
5. **Rolling mode in S13.** The text says "doubled rolling" on the finishing mill while calling the product two-side-bright.
6. **Temper.** H18 is the consensus; some vendor pages list O [VP S48].
7. **Pouch foil.** Toyo minima (75–85 MPa, 9–10 %) sit well below Chinese typical values; GB/T 22648 defaults to one-side-bright.
8. **GB/T 3615 low-voltage anode gauge.** 0.015–0.080 mm in one summary, 0.050–0.150 mm in another.

Your other priors hold: alloy, temper and gauge ranges, 600–1000 m/min, and 230–330 mm work rolls (confirmed only by vendor-level sources).
