## Rolling routes and reductions

**1. Brazed fin stock**
- **DC route [S3 F]:** hot band 3.5–4 mm → about 1 mm (71–75 %, multi-pass, calc.) → 0.123 mm (87.7 % calc.) → interanneal 350 °C × 4 h at 50 °C/h → 0.080 mm, which is 35 % final reduction.
- **Claimed windows:** final cold work 20–40 % [S3], or 20–35 % at 45–80 µm with interanneal at 275–400 °C [S5 F]. So the H14-type temper is set by a final reduction of roughly 20–40 % after the interanneal.
- **Result:** pre-braze UTS ≥210–220 MPa, post-braze 150–160 MPa, sag 21.5–33 mm [S3].
- **Interanneal temperature matters:** 500 °C instead of 350 °C gave finer post-braze grain and worse sag (32 mm against 24 mm) [S3].
- **Twin-roll-cast route [S4 F]:** cast 6–8 mm → 0.1–0.6 mm → interanneal 350–450 °C for 1–4 h → final reduction ≥45 % (60–90 % preferred) to 50–70 µm. Worked example: 0.109 → 0.060 mm (45.0 % calc.), interanneal 400 °C × 2 h.
- **Mitsubishi [S6 A]:** interanneal 460–600 °C for under 4 h, final reduction 15–50 %.
- **Thin-gauge limits:** a TRC Al-Si-Fe-Mn-Zn fin at 0.05 mm gives 130 MPa post-braze and about 50 % IACS, against 110 MPa and under 40 % IACS for 3xxx at 0.06 mm [S7]. Clad fin under 80 µm loses brazeability because filler diffuses into the core [S16 A]. Pre-braze strength must fall as gauge falls, for corrugation forming [S8 A].
- **3003 work hardening after a 420 °C/2 h anneal:** +23 MPa UTS going from 15 to 25 % reduction, and +31 MPa from 25 to 35 % [S40 A].

**2. Brazing sheet**
- Route: DC core, scalped, liner plates roll-bonded in hot rolling, cold rolled to gauge (0.27 mm in the example), final partial anneal to H24. Clad was 10 % 4343 plus 10 % waterside [S13 A].
- Heater-core tube is down to 0.2 mm [S2].
- No pass schedule was found.

**3. HVAC fin**
- **Full published sequence [S21 F]:** TRC 6.2 → 2.85 → 1.45 → 0.82 → (trim) → 0.42 → 0.23 mm, then foil passes 0.23 → 0.13 → 0.080–0.085 mm.
  - Reductions (calc.): 54.0 / 49.1 / 43.4 / 48.8 / 45.2 %, then 43.5 / 36.5 % (34.6–38.5 % across the final-gauge range).
  - Total 98.7 %, no interanneal.
  - Final anneal 265–272 °C × 25–30 h gave Rm 133–136 MPa, A 19–20 %, cupping 6.5 mm.
- **Novelis [S22 F]:** belt-cast 1.7 mm → 0.10 mm in three passes (94.1 % total, mean 61 % per pass, calc.). Partial anneal 245–255 °C with furnace overheat under 10 °C gave YS 100–111, UTS 119–126 MPa, A 17.5–18.5 %. The DC route is partially annealed at 240–270 °C at 0.10–0.13 mm.
- **8014-H22 [S23 A]:** 6.4–7.4 mm cast, homogenised 520–530 °C, eight passes to 0.17–0.24 mm (mean about 34–38 % per pass, calc.), final anneal 360–370 °C.

**4. Litho**
- **Hot band:** scalp 10–20 mm per face [S27 A]; homogenise 450–600 °C; hot roll to 2–7 mm (2.3–3.7 preferred), finishing at 280–350 °C [S25].
- **Cold rolling practice [S25 F]:** single-stand, one pass per coiling, coils cooled between passes, polished mill-finish rolls. Historically ≤50 % per pass, so at least four passes for 0.2–0.4 mm and five below 0.2 mm.
- **Hydro three-pass examples [S25 F] (gauges calc.):**

| Hot band (mm) | Pass reductions (%) | Gauges after each pass (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| 2.8 | 57 / 53 / 51 | 1.204 → 0.566 → 0.277 |
| 2.3 | 57 / 50 / 45 | 0.989 → 0.494 → 0.272 |
| 3.0 | 60 / 53 / 51 | 1.20 → 0.564 → 0.276 |

- **Limits stated [S25]:** first pass ≤65 % (≤60 % preferred), second ≤60 %, last ≤52 %. A 64 % first pass gave visibly worse pickling-test streakiness.
- **Interanneal:** optional, 230–490 °C for ≥1 h, usually before the last pass [S25]. Published gauges:
  - 0.9–1.2 mm → 0.28 mm (69–77 % calc.) [S26]
  - 2.0–2.4 mm → 0.29 mm (85.5–88 %) [S26]
  - 2.2 mm → 0.30 mm (86.4 %) [S27]
- **TRC route [S28 F]:** 4.5 → 1.5 mm (66.7 %) → recovery anneal 300 °C × 2 h → 0.28 mm (81.3 %). Gave 177 MPa, against 157 MPa with a recrystallising 380 °C anneal.
- **Surface:** conventional strip Ra 0.15–0.25 µm; last-pass work roll Ra 0.07–0.18 µm with ≥20 % reduction; peak height Rp ≤1.4 µm; degrease with pickling removal ≥0.25 g/m² [S34 A].
- **Flatness:** pure-stretch levelling line for 0.12–0.48 mm at up to 500 m/min [S35 V]; strip must be oil-free first [S35 V].

**Mill speeds (generic only):** single-stand cold mills 1,200–1,500 m/min maximum; foil mills up to 2,500 m/min [S37 V/A]. Strip reaches about 100 °C per pass, with 3–4 passes and coil cooling between [S36 F].

## Tolerances and standards

- **EN 683-1/-2/-3 (finstock) [S17 F, preview pages]:**
  - Scope 60–400 µm, unclad only.
  - 3003: H14 Rp ≥120, Rm 145–185, A ≥1 %; H16 ≥145, 165–205; H18 ≥160, ≥190; H19 ≥180, ≥210, A ≥0.5 %.
  - 1050A-H14: ≥85, 105–145.
  - Gauge: ±6 % single reading; average ±5 / ±4 / ±3 % for lots ≤3 t / 3–10 t / >10 t.
  - Width ±0.2 to ±1.0 mm.
  - The O/H22/H24 table was not read.
- **EN 485-4 [S18 A]:** applies above 0.20 mm. ±0.02 mm for 0.2–0.4 mm at ≤1000 mm width (soft-alloy group). Strip flatness is not specified.
- **Litho:** ordered as "EN 485-1 with special requirements" plus qualification [S19 A]. ISO 12635 plate thickness ±0.010 mm; CtP edge wave ≤3.0 mm (1.5 mm recommended) [S35b A]. Vendor strip tolerances ±0.005 mm (≤0.20) and ±0.007 mm (>0.20) [S36b V].
- **Flatness in I-units:** mill-exit targets 5–6 I-units for 1–3 mm strip, and ≤2 (down to 1) after stretch-bend levelling of thin strip [S41 A, a copper/thin-strip paper]. "<2 I-units" for litho appears only on a trade blog.
- **Not read:** EN 573-3, ASTM B209, JIS H4000/H4160, GB/T 3880 and YS/T 95.1 limits (catalogue records only) [M for anything beyond scope].

## Published causes of streak and band defects

**Litho**
- Coarse or mixed recrystallised grains in the hot band persist as rolling-direction streaks after graining. Remedy: no recrystallisation between hot passes, final hot pass ≥55 %, surface grain under 50 µm across the rolling direction [S29, S30 A].
- Texture bands (cube against copper orientation) etch differently [S32 A]; the interanneal controls streakiness [S31 A].
- Ingot origin: feather grains gave streaky sheet even after homogenising [S27]; fir-tree Al-Fe phase zones exposed by scalping; hydrogen content [S31].
- Cold rolling: excessive pass reduction tears the surface and leaves a streaky look [S25]; roll-grinding ridges and roll wear imprint into the strip [S34].
- Residual oil and sub-surface oxide islands leave un-grained plateaux [S31, S36b].

**General cold rolling (no fin-stock-specific study found)**
- Roll coating and pick-up re-transfer give rolled-in dirt and black streaks. Debris rises with reduction and roll roughness, moving from abrasive to adhesive wear [S33 A].
- Herringbone appears at ≥55 % per pass on hard alloys with ground rolls (stick-slip film breakdown); hard alloys are usually held to about 45 % per pass [S33].
- Heat stripes come from cross-width temperature or flatness differences; rolling stripes from local work-roll wear [S34b A].
- Mixed intermetallic phases in un-homogenised cast strip cause "smut" [S22].

## Disagreements between sources

- **Final reduction for fin:** 20–40 % (DC) vs 15–50 % vs ≥45–90 % (TRC).
- **Interanneal temperature for fin:** 200–400 °C vs 350–450 °C vs 460–600 °C.
- **Post-braze 3003:** Rm 110 / Rp 40 [S1] vs 122 / 67 [S10]. Sag tests use different overhangs (50 or 80 mm), so the values are not comparable.
- **Litho pass limit:** ≤50 % traditional vs 57–60 % (Hydro). Interanneal gauge ranges from 0.9 to 2.4 mm, or is omitted.
- **Litho thickness tolerance:** ±5–7 µm vs ±10 µm.
- **HVAC fin tolerance:** ±3 % vs ±5 % (vendors) vs ±6 % single reading (EN 683-3).
- **Fin gauge floor:** EN 683 stops at 60 µm, while 40–50 µm is sold.
- **Suspected typo:** S10 gives its sag test gauge as "0.008 mm"; it is almost certainly 0.08 mm.
