# Source and reference ledger

This is a claim-level index for the 2 October report addendum, not a replacement
for the 49-page report's 105-entry bibliography. “Depth” records what the
repository's documented review actually inspected. A DOI or abstract alone
does not supply parameters or validate a model. Publication metadata and exact
source limitations are recorded in the linked project documents.

| Source | Access depth recorded here | Supported use and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Erfanian, Brambley, Flanagan, O'Kiely & O'Connor, 2025, [DOI 10.1016/j.euromechsol.2025.105712](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.euromechsol.2025.105712); [2024 preprint v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.01347v1) | Journal metadata checked; preprint v1 equations 21–28 inspected. Journal equation numbering is unverified. | Leading-order rolling research motivation. The repository's varying-strength M1–M4 extension is separately derived and numerically tested; no aluminium validation follows. |
| Li, Matsumoto & Utsunomiya, 2018, [DOI 10.2355/isijinternational.ISIJINT-2017-623](https://doi.org/10.2355/isijinternational.ISIJINT-2017-623) | Primary full article and Eq. 1 inspected. | Circular Hitchcock M5 starting point and documented noncircular limit in stainless steel; no aluminium correction factor. |
| Shao, Sun, He & Li, 2015, [DOI 10.18280/ijht.330113](https://doi.org/10.18280/ijht.330113) | Primary HTML equations/tables and original PDF p. 94 inspected in the reviewed comparison. | Three AA5052 measured force/slip rows and stated process values. Temper, initial history, Eq. 3 accumulation coefficient, radius convention, speed location, instrument uncertainty and measured thermal state remain unresolved. |
| Olaogun, Edberg, Lindgren, Oluwole & Akinlabi, online 2018 / volume 2019, [DOI 10.1007/s00170-018-2811-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00170-018-2811-2) | Primary publisher abstract; full article unavailable in the recorded review. | AA8015 thermal/contact FE research lead only. No heat fraction, `c_p`, contact coefficient, roll mass or temperature validation transferred. E1–E4 are the repository's own declared first-law ledger. |
| Lenard & Zhang, 1997, [DOI 10.1016/S0924-0136(97)00183-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0924-0136(97)00183-0) | Publisher title/metadata; full text blocked in recorded local review. | Aluminium lubrication/friction lead. No coefficient or internal equation imported. Joint force, torque and measured slip would be needed to identify friction. |
| Hulamin, [5052 Data Sheet](https://www.hulamin.com/sites/default/files/downloads/5052%20Data%20Sheet.pdf) | Manufacturer one-page PDF text inspected in the comparison review. | Generic density/application context only; not the benchmark's temper or strength certificate. |
